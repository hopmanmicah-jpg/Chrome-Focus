About

Cars photographed the way they're built — precisely, and on purpose.

Chrome Focus is a Longmont, Colorado-based automotive photography studio. Every shoot is built around the vehicle's actual lines, stance, and light — not a filter trend. Studio, street, dealership, and track work, shot with the same eye for detail every time.

Available for individual owners, car clubs, dealerships, and event coverage across the Front Range.
